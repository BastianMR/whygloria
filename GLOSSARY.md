# Glossary

The only hand-maintained file in the navigation layer: everything else in
`navigation/` is generated by `python scripts/navigation.py`. Every term below
was checked against the corpus; the count is the number of concepts that use it.

| Term | Concepts | English gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AT | 33 | *Año Tributario*: the year a return belongs to. Income earned in year Y is declared in AT Y+1. |
| PPM | 49 | *Pago Provisional Mensual*: monthly prepayment of income tax. |
| RLI | 42 | *Renta Líquida Imponible*: taxable net income. |
| CPT | 18 | *Capital Propio Tributario*: tax equity, the starting point of the RLI. |
| FUT | 59 | *Fondo de Utilidades Tributables*: pre-2020 ledger of distributable taxed profits. |
| RAI | 28 | *Registro de Rentas Afectas a Impuestos*: post-2020 register of taxable earnings. |
| IDPC | 96 | *Impuesto de Primera Categoría*: corporate income tax. |
| IA | 77 | *Impuesto Adicional*: withholding on income earned by non-residents. |
| IVA | 804 | *Impuesto al Valor Agregado*: value added tax. |
| F22 | 34 | *Formulario 22*: annual income tax return. |
| F29 | 46 | *Formulario 29*: monthly return for VAT and PPM. |
| F50 | 16 | *Formulario 50*: annual return for second-category taxpayers. |
| F21 | 13 | *Formulario 21*: land contribution tax return. |
| DJ | 27 | *Declaración Jurada*: sworn statement, filed as a numbered form (`DJ1887`, ...). |
| UTM | 253 | *Unidad Tributaria Mensual*: monthly tax unit used for thresholds and penalties. |
| UTA | 211 | *Unidad Tributaria Anual*: twelve UTM. |
| UF | 102 | *Unidad de Fomento*: inflation-indexed unit used for monetary limits. |
| 14 A | 38 | *Artículo 14 letra A*: general regime (semi-integrated) for companies. |
| 14 D | 518 | *Artículo 14 letra D*: Pro Pyme regimes, numbered 1 to 8. |
| renta presunta | 82 | Presumptive income regime for small taxpayers in listed activities. |
| giro | 705 | *Giro*: the business activity registered with the SII. |
| domicilio | 725 | *Domicilio*: the tax address on record. |
| término de giro | 184 | *Término de giro*: formal cessation of activity, with a closing tax return. |
| boleta | 347 | *Boleta*: receipt issued to the final consumer. |
| factura | 462 | *Factura*: invoice issued between businesses, the basis of VAT credit. |
